Privacy Policy — Macro Dinner Planner

Last updated: 2026-09-28

This policy describes what data Macro Dinner Planner ("the app") collects, stores, and shares. It is written to match the App Store Connect App Privacy nutrition label exactly. If this document and the nutrition label ever disagree, the nutrition label is wrong — let us know at support@medbillresolve.com.

What the app collects

Macro Dinner Planner collects anonymous usage analytics only. When you have "Send anonymous usage stats" turned on in Settings → Privacy (default ON, easy to turn off), the app sends product-interaction events (e.g. "dinner_saved", "barcode_scanned"), crash diagnostics and performance data (collected through Apple's MetricKit), and basic context such as app version, iOS version, locale, and App Store country to PostHog (an analytics provider). We do not use session replay, autocapture, or person profiles, and the app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. These events are not linked to you — no name, email, recipe text, contacts, Apple ID, or advertising identifier is sent; events carry only a random identifier generated on your device for this install — and are not used to track you across apps, advertisers, or networks. Your saved dinners, recipes, goals, and barcode lookups themselves are stored only on your device, in the app's private Documents folder.

What the app stores locally

Saved dinners, your macro goals, and any barcode-scanned products are stored locally in Documents/MDP/dinners.json, goals.json, and barcode_foods.json. Removing the app from your device removes this data. The Settings tab also includes a one-tap "Reset all data" action.

What the app transmits

Everything you enter (recipes, goals, diary, edits) stays on your device. The app contacts the network only for these purposes:

  • Barcode lookup — when you tap Scan barcode, the scanned barcode number is sent to world.openfoodfacts.org to look up the product's nutrition info.
  • Food search — when you analyze a recipe or search foods, ingredient names and search text are sent to USDA FoodData Central (api.nal.usda.gov) and Open Food Facts to look up nutrition.
  • Recipe URL import — when you paste a recipe URL and tap Analyze, your device fetches that web page directly and parses it in memory. Nothing is logged to a server we control.
  • Anonymous usage analytics and crash diagnostics — sent to PostHog as described above, unless you turn off "Send anonymous usage stats".
  • Apple Search Ads attribution — on first launch the app asks Apple's AdServices framework for an anonymous attribution token so we can tell whether the install came from an Apple Search Ads campaign. No personal information is involved; you can turn off Apple's ad attribution in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

None of these requests include your name, email, contacts, or any account identifier.

Third-party services

Macro Dinner Planner uses a single third-party SDK: PostHog for the anonymous usage analytics described above (https://posthog.com/privacy). PostHog never receives your name, email, recipe text, contacts, or any user identifier. The other third-party endpoint contacted is the public Open Food Facts barcode lookup, and only when you tap Scan. No advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs are bundled.

Permissions we request

Camera — used only when you tap Scan barcode. Camera frames are decoded locally for the barcode value and never recorded or transmitted. No other system permissions are requested.

Your controls

  • Revoke any permission in iOS Settings → Macro Dinner Planner at any time.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally-stored data.
    • Account deletion: Macro Dinner Planner has no accounts. To remove all data the app has stored on your device, open Settings → "Reset all data" and confirm, or simply delete the app.
  • Contact us at support@medbillresolve.com to request any data we hold or to ask a question about this policy.

Children

Macro Dinner Planner is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app has no accounts, no advertising, and no social features. The only data it collects is the anonymous usage analytics and crash diagnostics described above, which are not linked to anyone's identity and are never used for tracking or advertising.

Changes

We'll update this page when the data we collect changes. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Material changes (new data types, new third-party services) will also prompt an in-app notice on next launch.
