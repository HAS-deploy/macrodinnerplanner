The app

Macro Dinner Planner is a free iOS application that helps users plan dinners against macronutrient goals. The app accepts typed or pasted recipes (or barcode-scanned packaged foods) and estimates calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat per ingredient and per dinner, then rolls those totals up against user-set daily or weekly targets.
