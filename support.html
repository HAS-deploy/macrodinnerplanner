Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. The app has no accounts, no logins, and no cloud sync. Everything stays on your device.
Where do the macro numbers come from?
Typed/pasted recipes are parsed locally and matched to a built-in food database (~150 staples). Barcode scans look up products from the public Open Food Facts database. All values are estimates, not medical advice.
How do I delete my data?
Open Settings inside the app and tap "Reset all data" to wipe every saved dinner, goal, and scan. You can also just delete the app — the data lives only on your device.
